# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript at the repository root. No framework and no build step. Confirmed by the request. Deployed by GitHub Pages from `main` at `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/Ink-and-Linen-Drawing-Studio-Website/`.

## Users

Two audiences, both named in the request. Artist prospects evaluate this as a sellable studio-site demo they could adapt. Visitors to the demo itself are people looking at paintings and drawings who may ask about a specific work, a commission, a workshop date, or a general question. Both act through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A generic, fictional painting and drawing studio site that shows how inquiries can move to WhatsApp without a cart or payment flow. Success means a prospect can see a complete, distinct studio page, and a visitor can inquire about a named work or a commission in one tap.

## Positioning

A warm sketchbook studio page, deliberately unlike the sibling bold dark editorial gallery at `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/Solenne-Vey-Art-Gallery-Website/`. Inquiries name the exact piece inside the WhatsApp draft. There is no shop.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on a phone or a laptop, filter works, open a piece, and continue the conversation in WhatsApp. The only contact channel provided is WhatsApp number 967783964784.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero, filterable works (Drawings, Watercolour, Oil/Acrylic, Sketchbook studies), lightbox, from-sketch-to-painting process, commissions, about, contact. A short workshops teaser is included (the request marked workshops optional; this build includes the teaser).
- Lightbox: arrow keys, Escape, and focus management.
- Every artwork card and the lightbox has an Inquire on WhatsApp link whose prefilled text names that piece. Commissions have Request a commission on WhatsApp. There is a general contact link and a floating WhatsApp button. No other phone number.
- Commissions cover portraits, pet portraits, and custom pieces. The process is described. Prices are not shown. The words used are “Quote on request”.
- No cart and no payments.
- Responsive from 360px up, with no horizontal scroll.
- Lighthouse performance and SEO aimed at 90 or above.
- Title, meta description, Open Graph image, alt text, semantic HTML, SVG favicon, visible focus, accessible contrast, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- Assumption, delegated by the request (“fictional artist name of your choice”): the artist is Mara Ellison.
- Assumption: no email address and no street address were given, so none are invented. Contact is WhatsApp only.
- The artist is fictional and the page says so.

## Brand Commitments

The request binds these and they are recorded without expansion: the name Ink & Linen Studio; a warm, hand-made sketchbook feel; paper and linen textures in CSS; ink and graphite tones with one warm accent; a handwritten-style display face paired with a clean serif; subtle pencil-stroke and underline SVG animation; gently tilted pinned-sketch cards; a look clearly different from the dark editorial sibling demo.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository started with a one-line README. No photographs, prices, testimonials, press, or client lists were supplied. Artwork will be 9 to 12 downloaded Unsplash or Pexels images, credited in the footer, used as stand-ins for a fictional practice. Do not invent prices, client counts, press, or a claim that a real person is Mara Ellison.

## Product Principles

- The work is the first thing a visitor meets, and the next step is a named WhatsApp inquiry.
- Say the artist is fictional. Credit every photograph.
- Quote on request. Never imply a price, a cart, or a payment.
- One WhatsApp number, written the same way everywhere.
- The page must stay readable and usable with a keyboard, on a narrow phone, and with motion reduced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Required by the request: semantic HTML, text alternatives, contrast that meets ordinary body-text needs, a visible focus state, a lightbox that can be used from the keyboard and then returns focus, and motion that yields to `prefers-reduced-motion`.
