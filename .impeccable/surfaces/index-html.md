# Ink & Linen Studio

## Scope
Public landing page for the fictional studio. Visitor mode: experience. The drawing leads the first viewport. The working action is a WhatsApp note that already names the piece.

## Audience and job
Artist prospects judging a sellable demo, and visitors who want to ask about a work, a commission, or a workshop date. Proof on the page is the work itself, the blank price line, and the named inquiry. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, stock photographs only, one WhatsApp number, no prices, no cart.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a colourman’s sample card opened on a linen-bound sketchbook. Each work is a painted-out sample with the price left blank. It refuses both the centered artist-template and the sibling dark editorial grid.
OWN-WORLD: Rag paper and a linen weave own the ground, the nav, and the controls. Graphite for reading, one walnut-sepia for the stamp and the pencil. Shantell Sans speaks only in the artist’s voice. Source Serif 4 speaks only in the reading voice. A chosen medium is an unbroken pencil stroke. An unchosen one is a gapped stroke. Studies are pinned, taped, and slightly tilted. The sheet in hand is the lightest paper.
STORY: A visitor meets a large charcoal study, reads whose studio it is, and can send a WhatsApp note that names that drawing. They filter the sample card by medium, open a sheet, and inquire. Commissions are a filled-in sheet whose price line says Quote on request.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One open book, one gutter. Left page: a wall-scale pinned drawing, tape at the top, handwritten title, medium, Quote on request, and a stamp that inquires about that exact piece. Right page: the studio name in the artist’s hand, one short paragraph, and a text link down to the next leaf. The margin index (Work, Process, Commissions, About, Contact) sits on the top edge of the book. No label above the heading.
FORM: Colourman’s sample card, third grounded candidate, seed 4ed997aa. Materials translated onto the pinned sketchbook: paper and linen in CSS, ink and graphite, one warm accent, handwritten display, clean serif, tilted pinned studies, pencil-stroke SVG.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
Choosing a medium draws a graphite stroke under that word. The same stroke sits under the title in the lightbox.
